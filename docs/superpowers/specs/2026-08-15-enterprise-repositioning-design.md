# Round Digital — Enterprise & Public-Sector Repositioning

**Design specification** · 2026-08-15

---

## 1. Executive summary

Round Digital operates today as an SMB-focused boutique. The homepage eyebrow reads
"For ambitious small & mid-sized businesses" and the FAQ opens with "We're a small
business, not an enterprise — are we a fit?". That copy is well written and entirely
wrong for the buyer we now want.

This spec repositions the firm as a **women-owned technology and workforce services
company** selling five linked capabilities to government and enterprise buyers across
Canada, the United States and Asia. It keeps the `round.digital` domain and its indexed
authority, retargets the existing 20 service and industry pages, and adds a
public-sector section built for procurement evaluation rather than lead generation.

The strategy rests on one structural claim: **we are not a smaller TCS.** We do not win
on headcount and should never invite that comparison. We win as the certified,
fast-moving specialist that primes small and mid-sized contracts, subcontracts to the
large integrators on everything else, and closes a delivery loop none of them close.

### The one-sentence positioning

> Round Digital builds the system, staffs it, trains the people who run it, and measures
> whether it worked — so the capability is still there after we leave.

### What must be true before launch

Four items block publication of bid-facing pages. They are tracked in §12.

1. Ownership and control of record must show Rainu Singh as majority owner.
2. The SOC 2 Type II claim on `perfectum.ai` must be substantiated or removed.
3. Headcount must be stated consistently across all properties.
4. The "Toronto, Dallas & Pune" location claim must be made true or corrected.

## 3. Positioning and message architecture

### 3.1 The spine

The five pillars are not five businesses. They are one lifecycle, and the lifecycle is
the differentiator:

> **Build it. Staff it. Train it. Prove it.** — AI enablement runs through all four.

| Pillar | Role in the loop |
|---|---|
| **AI Enablement** | The strategy and the connective layer |
| **IT Services** | Build and run the systems |
| **Staffing** | Put capable people in the seats |
| **Training** | Make them capable — the Pragra heritage |
| **Research & Data** | Measure whether any of it worked |

Large integrators sell towers and leave. Nobody hires their way out of an AI skills gap
and nobody consults their way out either. The loop is true of our actual service mix,
which is what makes it defensible under evaluation.

### 3.2 Why Research & Data is the sleeper pillar

The Foundations for Evidence-Based Policymaking Act of 2018 obligates US federal
agencies to build evidence for their programs. That is statutory, funded, recurring
demand for survey research and program evaluation. **NAICS 541910** is actively
competed and materially less crowded than 541512, where every IT shop in the country
is stacked up.

Expect the first public-sector award to come through 541910 or 611430, not through IT.
The site must therefore give Research & Data and Training equal visual weight to IT —
not treat them as adjacent services.

### 3.3 Rainu Singh as the authority asset

Twenty years in **audit and QA** is not generic executive tenure. It maps precisely onto
the single hottest unmet need in enterprise AI: **assurance**. Who checks that the model
does what it claims, that the data lineage holds, that the controls are evidenced?

This is the content-authority play. Nobody owns "AI assurance and audit" as a voice.
Rainu Singh can, because she has the twenty years to back it. It pairs with ISO 42001
(§8) and NIST AI RMF work to make the AI Enablement pillar credible in a way that
scale-based competitors cannot answer.

Byline her on the assurance and governance guides. Named, credentialed authorship is
also the E-E-A-T signal Google needs on this topic cluster.

### 3.4 Handling women-owned

Two placements, deliberately separated:

- **As a procurement instrument** — on `/government` and `/government/teaming`:
  supplier-diversity spend credit, WBE Canada eligibility, corporate diversity program
  qualification. Concrete, mechanical, buyer-relevant.
- **As a leadership story** — on `/about/women-owned` and `/about/leadership`: Rainu
  Singh's record, the operating philosophy, the Pragra outcomes.

Never as a decorative footer badge. Evaluators discount decoration and reward
specificity.

### 3.5 Voice

Declarative. Specific. Numbers with denominators. No "leverage", no "seamless", no
"cutting-edge", no "in today's fast-paced digital landscape". The existing site's copy is
already good on this axis — the failure is targeting, not craft. Preserve the craft.

Prohibited: any claim we cannot document if a contracting officer asks for the file.

## 4. Market strategy by geography

### 4.1 Canada — primary near-term market

Rainu Singh's Canadian citizenship or PR status makes this the only market where the
women-owned credential converts **today**.

| Move | Detail |
|---|---|
| **WBE Canada certification** | 51% owned, managed and controlled by women who are Canadian citizens or PRs. She qualifies. ~60–90 days. |
| **CanadaBuys supplier registration + Procurement Business Number** | Prerequisite to everything federal |
| **PSPC supply arrangements** | **TBIPS** (Task-Based Informatics Professional Services) is the primary federal IT services vehicle and is genuinely achievable at our size. Also **ProServices** (under $3.75M) and **SBIPS**. |
| **OSME** (Office of Small and Medium Enterprises) | Actively runs seminars and helps SMEs bid. Free. Use it. |
| **Innovative Solutions Canada** | Funds and buys from Canadian SMEs — direct fit for the AI pillar |
| **Federal Supplier Diversity Action Plan** | PSPC's commitment to increase procurement from under-represented suppliers |

Existing Ontario delivery and the Forbes recognition make this credible immediately.

### 4.2 United States — small business, not diversity set-asides

**Closed to us** (all require US-citizen ownership): WOSB, EDWOSB, 8(a), SDB self-cert,
HUBZone.

**Open to us:** plain small-business status. 13 CFR 121.105 requires a business
"organized for profit, with a place of business located in the United States, and which
operates primarily within the United States or which makes a significant contribution to
the U.S. economy" — **no ownership-citizenship test.** Small business set-asides are by
far the largest set-aside category. That is the pool.

This makes the **US delivery footprint the highest-ROI item on the roadmap.** Today,
Wyoming-registered with all delivery in Canada and India, the 121.105 test is not
convincingly met and much federal work is unreachable regardless:

- Many solicitations require US-performed work.
- Anything touching CUI triggers DFARS 252.204-7012 and NIST SP 800-171, commonly with
  US-persons restrictions.
- No facility clearance means no classified work.

**Recommendation.** Stand up a real US delivery centre — office and US-based staff —
sited for talent cost and state-vehicle access. Texas (DIR contract vehicle,
Dallas/Austin/San Antonio talent) and Virginia (VITA, proximity to federal buyers) are
the strongest candidates. Do not site for HUBZone; that certification is closed to us.

**Sequence:** US state & local and commercial enterprise first (no US-performance
restrictions of the federal kind, much lower barrier), US federal as subcontractor
second, US federal as small-business prime third.

### 4.3 Asia

Delivery advantage (India), not a bid market near-term. Two roles:

- **Delivery credibility** — the Noida centre is a cost and follow-the-sun argument for
  commercial buyers, and must be presented with the security posture to match.
- **WEConnect International** certification opens Asia-Pacific corporate supplier
  diversity programs — the one channel where women-owned status monetises across all
  three target regions at once.

### 4.4 Commercial supplier diversity — the cross-region unlock

Large corporates run supplier diversity programs with real spend targets and generally
accept WBENC **or** WBE Canada **or** WEConnect certification. This channel is open in
all three regions, has no citizenship barrier through the Canadian and international
certifiers, and is faster-closing than any government pursuit.

**Treat it as a first-class revenue channel, not a compliance checkbox.** It gets its own
page: `/government/teaming` covers public-sector teaming; supplier diversity for
corporates is addressed on `/about/women-owned` with a direct enquiry path.

## 8. Certification roadmap

Sequenced by leverage per dollar. Every US-citizenship-gated program is excluded.

### Tier 0 — free, ~30 days

1. **Complete the SBA DSBS profile.** This is what US contracting officers actually
   search, and almost nobody fills it in properly. Free.
2. **Audit SAM.gov NAICS/PSC** — add 541910, 611430, 611420, 561320, 561311.
3. **CanadaBuys** supplier registration + Procurement Business Number.
4. **Engage OSME** — free SME bidding support.
5. **NIST SP 800-171 self-assessment** and post the SPRS score. Free, and a prerequisite
   for any future DoD-adjacent work.

### Tier 1 — 60–120 days

| Certification | Cost / Time | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **WBE Canada** | ~$500 · 60–90d | The women-owned credential that works today. Canadian federal + corporate diversity. |
| **WEConnect International** | Low · ~60d | Asia-Pacific and global corporate supplier diversity. Third market, one application. |
| **ISO 9001:2015** | $8–20k · 3–6mo | **Highest-leverage certification we can hold.** A QMS is Rainu Singh's professional signature, not a purchase. Scored evaluation factor in most solicitations. |
| **ISO/IEC 27001:2022** | $15–40k · 4–8mo | Effectively mandatory for enterprise and public-sector IT |
| **WBENC** | ~$350–1,250 · 60–90d | **Conditional** — WBENC requires US citizenship or lawful permanent residency. Verify Rainu Singh's US immigration status against the current standard before applying. |

### Tier 2 — 6–18 months

- **ISO/IEC 42001:2023 — AI Management System.** The first AI governance standard.
  Almost no services firm holds it. This credentials the AI Enablement pillar in a way
  scale cannot answer, pairs with NIST AI RMF for federal buyers, and sits directly on
  Rainu Singh's audit background. **Strongest single differentiator available to us.**
- **SOC 2 Type I → Type II** — required to sell Perfectum into enterprise, and the
  correct resolution of the live claim on `perfectum.ai`.
- **CMMC Level 1 (self-assessment)** → **Level 2 (C3PAO)** — the door to DoD.
- **ISO 20000-1** if pursuing managed services and O&M.

### Tier 3 — 12–36 months

- **GSA MAS**, SINs 54151S · 541910 · 611430. Requires 2 years in business, financials
  and past performance. The single largest US federal unlock.
- **State term contracts** — Texas DIR, Virginia VITA, California CMAS, NASPO
  ValuePoint. A fraction of the federal barrier with real money behind them. Pursue in
  parallel with, not after, GSA.
- **CMMI-DEV/SVC Level 3** — $50–150k. A Year-3 move. Frequently a hard gate in large
  federal IT, and not worth the capital before then.

### Closed to us — do not pursue, do not imply

WOSB · EDWOSB · 8(a) Business Development · SDB self-certification · HUBZone. All
require 51% ownership by US citizens. Revisit only if ownership citizenship changes.

## 10. Design direction

### The problem with the current design

Space Mono headlines, 24–40px corner radii, image collages and floating animations read
as *creative studio*. That is the correct language for an SMB agency and the wrong
language for a procurement evaluator or a Fortune 500 vendor review. The current visual
system actively contradicts the positioning we are adopting.

### The target

Reference the presentation standard of Booz Allen, ICF, Maximus and Accenture Federal —
not their aesthetics, their **information density and document-grade structure**.

| Dimension | Current | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Headline type | Space Mono | A serious grotesk, or a transitional serif for editorial contrast. Mono retreats to data, codes and metrics — where it earns its keep. |
| Corner radius | 24–40px | 8–12px. Softness reads as consumer. |
| Density | Generous whitespace, large imagery | Higher information density. Tables, specification blocks, credential modules. Evaluators want to find facts fast. |
| Imagery | Stock collages | Reduce sharply. Replace with data, diagrams, credential marks and real delivery artefacts. |
| Colour | Accent-led | Restrained, high-contrast, one confident accent. Must pass WCAG AA at every pairing. |
| Motion | GSAP reveals throughout | Retain, but reduce amplitude. Honour `prefers-reduced-motion`. |

### New components required

`CredentialGrid` · `NaicsTable` · `CapabilityStatementBlock` (print-optimised) ·
`ContractVehicleCard` · `PastPerformanceCard` (challenge / approach / outcome / period
of performance) · `ComplianceMatrix` · `LocationCard` · `DownloadCTA`.

### Accessibility is a positioning asset

**WCAG 2.1 AA is non-negotiable.** Section 508 conformance is scored on US federal
digital work, and the Accessible Canada Act applies federally in Canada. Our own site is
the first evidence a buyer sees of whether we can deliver it. Publish an accessibility
statement and mean it. Most competitors at our size cannot make this claim.

### 12.1 Ownership alignment — required sequence

Certifiers do not audit public profiles. They audit documents, then interview. Work in
this order; reversing it manufactures the discrepancy the exercise is meant to remove.

**Step 1 — Documentary record.** Articles of incorporation · share register and share
certificates · cap table · minute book · bylaws reviewed specifically for supermajority
or veto provisions · board resolutions · bank signing authority · contract and bid
signature authority · proof of Canadian citizenship or PR.

**Step 2 — Operating reality.** Officer titles, board composition and actual decision
rights must match the documents.

**Step 3 — Public record.** LinkedIn, Crunchbase, Forbes, the website. Last, and only
after steps 1 and 2 hold.

#### The control test is where applications fail

51% ownership alone does not satisfy certification. The woman owner must demonstrably
control the business:

| Criterion | Requirement | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Highest officer position | President or CEO — not COO, not VP, not "co-founder" | **Open** — Atin Singh currently listed as CEO |
| Board control | Majority of directors; no minority veto in the bylaws | Verify |
| Signing authority | Bank accounts, contracts and bid submissions | Verify |
| Industry expertise | Must be able to run the firm independently | **Satisfied** — 20 years IT audit and QA |
| Compensation | Consistent with being the controlling owner | Verify |

If Atin Singh remains CEO, the application fails on control regardless of the share
register. This is an operating decision, not a paperwork one.

#### The independence test — a second risk specific to this structure

Round Digital, Pragra LLC and Perfectum AI are visibly entangled; Perfectum AI's LinkedIn
still reads "formerly PRAGRA LLC". Certifiers may deny on **independence** if the applicant
depends on an entity controlled by a non-qualifying person for financing, contracts, staff
or premises. The corporate tree must be untangled and documented before applying.

#### Timing

An ownership transfer executed immediately before application draws scrutiny. If Rainu
Singh has held majority ownership throughout and the record was simply never updated,
that must be evidenced with dated documents. Confirm this before filing anything.

`next/image` migration, generated sitemap. Homepage, `/government` hub,
`/government/capability-statement`, `/about/women-owned`, `/about/leadership`. Tier 0
certifications filed in parallel — they cost nothing and take 30 days.

**Phase 2 — Pillars (weeks 5–9).** Five pillar hubs and their children. Route
migrations and redirects. Structured data. `/products`. Locations.

**Phase 3 — Authority (weeks 10–14).** `/insights/guides` cluster, Rainu Singh's bylined
assurance and governance series, case studies, industry page retargeting including the
four new public-sector sectors.

**Phase 4 — Ongoing.** Tier 1 and Tier 2 certifications, partnership applications,
capability statement kept current, past-performance library grown to five references.
